Be, moods & introductions

Le verbe be, les humeurs, les questions et la présentation d'un personnage, pour le test d'anglais.

La leçon

Le verbe be (être)

Forme affirmativeForme négative
I am = I'mI am not = I'm not
You areYou aren't
He isHe isn't
She isShe isn't
It isIt isn't
We areWe aren't
You areYou aren't
They areThey aren't

isn't = is not, aren't = are not. Attention : « I amn't » n'existe pas, on dit I'm not.

Un prénom seul → is (Jenny is happy). Deux personnes → are (Mike and Paul are sick).

L'apostrophe remplace les lettres enlevées : I'm, isn't, aren't, what's. Et I s'écrit toujours en majuscule.

Moods (les humeurs)

Touche pour écouter.

How do you feel? → I feel great. / I'm tired.
Gabriel is grumpy today.
Sally is sick, she has got a cold. (Sally est malade, elle a un rhume.)

Se présenter : Hi. I'm Paul and I'm eleven.

Questions et réponses

    Présenter un personnage

    He is Amar. He is ten (years old). He is Welsh. He is happy, he isn't sad.

    She is Jenny. She is fourteen. She is American.

    They are Kate and Meg. They are seven. They are Australian. They aren't Irish.

    Les nationalités prennent une majuscule : American, Welsh, French.
